# research sources

sources.jsonl logs every source consulted while building and verifying the
market intelligence tables, with the date it was fetched, what it supports,
its license terms and a boundary statement of what it does not support.

the discipline in short:

- official postal-operator and carrier pages beat blogs, always.
- a row is only marked verified when the official page actually confirms
  the service and, where claimed, the address format. rows where only the
  service was confirmed say so in their notes.
- every carrier row carries a re-verification cadence; carrier formats
  change on the carrier's schedule. australia post changed its parcel
  locker format in 2026 and kept the old format working, which is why the
  au rows re-verify every 90 days.
- absence from this list means unverified, never disproven.
